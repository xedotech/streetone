---

## Short pitch

Closed studios spend a decade before you touch the stick. Street One ships a playable street tonight — source in the zip, no store, no season pass.

The north star is a free game at the scale people actually want: an open city, working guns and vehicles, PC first, consoles when the build and the licenses exist. We are not those commercial giants. We are the open alternative, started from one block.

Play it. If it hits, donate. If it does not, the street still compiles.

---

## The story

See what we can do, then decide.

You spawn in the driver seat. W throttle. S brake. **A left. D right.** F hops you onto the asphalt. Space fires. Six AI chase and return fire. Clear the block or drop. That is the proof, today.

Everything else — a district, native Windows/Linux, pad-first console exports, online sessions — is a public line item, not a trailer promise.

AI-built first cut. MIT. Community-owned. Nobody can take the repo private.

## What’s in the demo (already)

- One night city street
- Arcade car, enter / exit
- On-foot pistol vs six simple AI
- Win / lose
- Godot 4 source + WebGL port of the same loop

## What we will not claim

That we have shipped a finished open world, a live-service shooter, or a console SKU. Naming the closed giants is the ambition. The street is the evidence.
